## Limits retained

Local personal-project backend, not frontend or deployed service. Runtime first-admin provisioning/production infrastructure is explicitly outside scope. Foreign eligibility is decision-time through owner contracts, not cross-module write locks; direct SQL is not an audited business interface. Event admission has no implicit time cutoff, waitlist or fee; Library no reservations/renewals/fines/self-borrow. No SMTP/SMS, external providers, auto Finance/Notification coupling, global audit timeline/export/expiry, benchmark or coverage percentage. Broad substring/unfiltered history queries may scan. Later Reporting, frontend, E2E release and optional workflow/AI still require their own approved phases; this goal must stop after the Phase 5 backend gates.
